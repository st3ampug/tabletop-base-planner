Tabletop Base Planner

How to use the planner

Plan deployments and moves with true-size bases on the 11th-edition layouts. Everything is measured in inches, base edge to base edge, and saves itself in this browser.

Using the planner

The board

  • Layout: tap the layout name at the top. Pick your Force Disposition (row) against your opponent's (column), then one of the variants.
  • Moving: drag a base to move it. The distance moved shows in inches. The Drag switch at the top right of the board chooses between moving the whole unit and one model.
  • View: drag the table to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, and press Fit to see the whole board.
  • Ruler: turn it on and drag anywhere on the board to measure.
  • Warnings: a red dashed outline marks a base in an illegal position (overlapping a wall or another base). An orange dashed outline marks a model out of unit coherency.
  • Sight: the Sight button on the board tints red everywhere the watched units can see, so the clear areas are out of their sight. Pick units to watch with the eye buttons in the Armies tab; turning Sight on with nothing watched watches the whole hostile army. With one of your units selected, the clear areas shrink so its biggest base fits there wholly out of sight.

The tabs

  • Armies: paste a list exported from the Warhammer app, or written as text, and press Read list. Fill in anything marked red (vehicles without a base need a hull size, which is remembered), then use it as the friendly or hostile army. Here you also set each army's name, colour and zone, swap sides, deploy or remove units, watch units for the Sight overlay (the eye buttons), and edit the roster by hand.
  • Selected: the tapped base or terrain piece. Turn oval and hull bases, change the label or colour, show a range ring around the model or unit (1" to 12", or any distance), duplicate or remove.
  • Bases: add loose bases by size, for models that aren't in a list.
  • Terrain: turn on Edit terrain to drag pieces, or add new ones. Each layout keeps its own changes, and Restore layout defaults undoes them.
  • Rules: choose whether walls stop bases or only flag them, whether bases block each other, the coherency distance, and what blocks sight and how far it reaches. The defaults are 10th-edition values; set them to your 11th-edition cards.
  • Save: keep several named boards, copy a board as text to move it to another device or give it to an analysis AI, or open a saved file.

Keys

  • Ctrl+Z undo · arrow keys nudge the selection 1 mm, with Shift 1" · R turn 15° · D duplicate · Delete remove

Through a game

  1. Pick the layout. Agree Force Dispositions with your opponent and choose the matching layout.
  2. Load both armies. Import your list as friendly and your opponent's as hostile in the Armies tab. Each army deploys into its zone straight away.
  3. Set the sides. The attacker deploys in the red zone at the top. If you're on the wrong side, press Swap sides.
  4. Deploy. Drag units into position. Use range rings to check distances to the enemy and objectives, and clear any red or orange warnings.
  5. Keep the deployment. In the Save tab, press Duplicate this board and rename the copy, e.g. "Deployment", so you can come back to it.
  6. Play each turn. Before moving, select the unit and turn on Sight to see where it can end its move out of the enemy's view. Move units with the distance readout and the ruler, check ranges with rings, and remove destroyed units from the Selected tab. Duplicate the board at the end of a turn to keep a record, e.g. "Round 2, hostile".
  7. Review. Copy any board as text from the Save tab. It includes the table layout, so an analysis AI can read the positions.

Recording a game round by round, with wounds and replay, is planned. Until then, a board per turn keeps the history.

Collision rules

Each terrain piece has its own rule. By default terrain areas (pale, dashed) can be entered and walls, barriers, pipes and generators (dark) block. Tap any piece on the board to switch it.

Unit coherency

" of 2 others in units of+

Defaults are the 10th-edition values: within 2" of one other model, two others for units of 7 or more. Set them to your 11th-edition card. Distances are measured base edge to base edge.

Line of sight

Turn on Sight on the board to tint red everywhere the watched units can see. Pick the units with the eye buttons in the Armies tab.

Walls and solid scenery always block sight. Areas block it like 10th-edition ruins: models see into an area and out of it, not through it. Set this to your 11th-edition card. Range is measured from the watching model's base edge. There are no terrain heights, and models don't block sight.

Boards

Each board keeps its own layout, armies and positions, and saves itself in this browser.

Save and load

To move a board to another device, copy the text below and load it there. A map file loads the same way. The copied text also describes the table (zones, objective centres, terrain and walls in mm) for anyone analysing it.
